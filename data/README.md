# Data and attribution

`curated/` contains original project summaries and translations derived from linked official information. Individual rows retain source identifiers, and the source catalog identifies relevant sections. `fixtures/` contains fictional network observations; these are not healthcare facts.

No patient records, student identifiers, insurance member numbers, or personal symptom histories are included. Facility addresses and phone numbers are public institutional contact information. Blank fields mean they were not collected or verified, not that the facility has no such information.

The raw HTML/PDF acquisition is reproducible with `python -m clearcare check-sources`, subject to the websites' current availability. Full source pages are downloaded only into ignored local folders. The initial public retrieval log contains URLs, byte hashes, dates, and short retrieval checks; it is not a complete archive or a substitute for reading the sources.

Original project code, summaries, and translations are covered by the repository's MIT License. Original linked pages, institutional names, and third-party content retain their own terms and ownership. The project is not affiliated with or endorsed by UIUC, McKinley, Carle, CMS, or NIH.
