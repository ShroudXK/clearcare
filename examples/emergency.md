# ClearCare: emergency entry

For a medical emergency, call 911 or seek the nearest emergency department. Do not delay emergency care for these appointment or insurance steps.

医疗紧急情况请拨打 911 或寻求最近的急诊科帮助。不要为完成预约或保险步骤而延误急诊。

[Official source](https://www.healthcare.gov/using-marketplace-coverage/getting-emergency-care/)
