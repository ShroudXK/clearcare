## Preparation checklist

- [ ] **Understand the care entry points / 理解不同就医入口**
  - Review official descriptions of primary care, walk-in care, and emergency departments. These labels describe services; this guide does not choose a care level from symptoms.
  - 查看官方对 primary care、walk-in care 和 emergency department 的说明。本指南解释服务类型，不根据症状判断应去哪类机构。
  - Type: education · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Get Care](https://www.carle.org/home/get-care) (Care types; CARE line; patient information line; reviewed 2026-10-07)
- [ ] **Ask a healthcare professional when unsure / 不确定时咨询医疗人员**
  - McKinley lists a 24/7 nurse phone service at 217-333-2700. Ask whether you are eligible to use it.
  - McKinley 公布了 24/7 护士电话服务：217-333-2700。请确认自己是否有资格使用。
  - Type: action · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Dial-a-Nurse](https://mckinley.illinois.edu/get-healthcare/dial-nurse) (Dial-a-Nurse; reviewed 2026-10-07)
- [ ] **Start with your existing follow-up instructions / 先查看已有复诊要求**
  - Use the follow-up instructions from your treating clinician. Contact that team to clarify the next visit and which specialist to contact. This guide does not set a treatment timetable.
  - 先查看治疗医生提供的 follow-up instructions；向原治疗团队确认下一次就诊和应联系的专科。本指南不设定治疗或复诊时间。
  - Type: action · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Be Prepared for Your Visit, Step 6](https://www.cms.gov/marketplace/outreach-and-education/downloads/c2c-prepare-for-your-visit.pdf) (PDF pages 4-7 (one-based); reviewed 2026-10-07)
- [ ] **Identify your exact insurance plan / 确认具体保险计划**
  - Use your insurance documents or contact your insurer to identify the plan and its provider directory. An insurer name alone is not enough to confirm a provider network.
  - 查看保险文件或联系保险公司，确认具体 plan 及 provider directory。仅知道保险公司名称，不足以确认医生属于哪个网络。
  - Type: question · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Getting regular medical care](https://www.healthcare.gov/using-marketplace-coverage/getting-medical-care/) (Finding a doctor in your plan; reviewed 2026-10-07)
- [ ] **Check the provider, location, and plan together / 同时核实医生、地点和保险计划**
  - Check your plan directory and confirm with the insurer and office. Ask about the named clinician and visit location. Network participation is separate from coverage of a particular service.
  - 查看计划目录，并向保险公司和诊所确认具体医生与就诊地点。属于网络内，不等于某项服务一定获得 coverage。
  - Type: question · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Getting regular medical care](https://www.healthcare.gov/using-marketplace-coverage/getting-medical-care/) (Finding a doctor in your plan; reviewed 2026-10-07)
- [ ] **Ask whether a referral is needed / 确认是否需要转诊单**
  - Ask the insurer and specialist office whether this visit requires a referral. A referral is an order to see a specialist or receive certain services; requirements vary.
  - 向保险公司与专科诊所确认这次就诊是否需要 referral。Referral 是转诊至专科或某些服务的医嘱；要求会因情况而异。
  - Type: question · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Referral](https://www.healthcare.gov/glossary/referral/) (Glossary definition; reviewed 2026-10-07)
- [ ] **Ask about prior authorization for planned services / 询问计划中的服务是否需要预授权**
  - For planned services, ask whether prior authorization is required and who submits it. Preauthorization is not a promise that the plan will cover the cost.
  - 对于计划中的服务，询问是否需要 prior authorization，以及由谁申请。获得预授权不代表保险一定支付费用。
  - Type: question · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Preauthorization](https://www.healthcare.gov/glossary/preauthorization/) (Glossary definition; reviewed 2026-10-07)
- [ ] **Ask what costs remain your responsibility / 确认需要自付的费用**
  - Ask your insurer how the deductible and other cost sharing apply to the planned visit. This project does not estimate a final bill.
  - 向保险公司询问 deductible 和其他费用分担如何适用于这次就诊。本项目不估算最终账单。
  - Type: question · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Deductible](https://www.healthcare.gov/glossary/deductible/) (Glossary definition; reviewed 2026-10-07)
- [ ] **Confirm the booking route and availability / 确认预约方式和是否接诊**
  - Use the official care entry or office contact. Say whether you are a new patient and explain the visit purpose. Ask whether that visit type can be booked online.
  - 使用官方就医入口或联系办公室。说明是否为新患者以及就诊目的；确认该类就诊是否能在线预约。
  - Type: question · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Get Care](https://www.carle.org/home/get-care) (Care types; CARE line; patient information line; reviewed 2026-10-07); [Be Prepared for Your Visit, Step 6](https://www.cms.gov/marketplace/outreach-and-education/downloads/c2c-prepare-for-your-visit.pdf) (PDF pages 4-7 (one-based); reviewed 2026-10-07)
- [ ] **Request an interpreter ahead of time / 提前询问口译支持**
  - Call the office before the visit to request an interpreter. Confirm arrangements; this project does not assume every location has the same language services.
  - 就诊前联系办公室申请 interpreter，并确认安排。本项目不假设每个地点提供相同的语言服务。
  - Type: question · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Talking With Your Doctor](https://medlineplus.gov/talkingwithyourdoctor.html) (Summary; reviewed 2026-10-07)
- [ ] **Ask how to transfer existing records / 询问如何提交已有病历**
  - Ask the receiving office which existing records it needs and how to send them. McKinley uses an authorization process for record requests; allow time for processing.
  - 向接收诊所确认需要哪些已有病历及提交方式。McKinley 申请病历需要授权流程，应留出处理时间。
  - Type: question · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Be Prepared for Your Visit, Step 6](https://www.cms.gov/marketplace/outreach-and-education/downloads/c2c-prepare-for-your-visit.pdf) (PDF pages 4-7 (one-based); reviewed 2026-10-07); [Medical Records](https://mckinley.illinois.edu/medical-services/medical-records) (Requesting information from the McKinley EHR; reviewed 2026-10-07)
- [ ] **Prepare identification and visit documents / 准备身份与就诊文件**
  - Bring available insurance documentation, photo identification, and any requested forms. Ask the office about payment requirements.
  - 准备已有保险凭证、带照片的身份证明及诊所要求的表格，并确认付款要求。
  - Type: action · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Be Prepared for Your Visit, Step 6](https://www.cms.gov/marketplace/outreach-and-education/downloads/c2c-prepare-for-your-visit.pdf) (PDF pages 4-7 (one-based); reviewed 2026-10-07)
- [ ] **Prepare medication and allergy information / 准备用药与过敏信息**
  - Make a list of medicines, supplements, allergies, and questions. Keep the list with you rather than entering personal medical details into this demo.
  - 整理药物、补充剂、过敏情况和问题清单。自行携带，不需要在本演示中输入个人医疗详情。
  - Type: action · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Talking With Your Doctor](https://medlineplus.gov/talkingwithyourdoctor.html) (Summary; reviewed 2026-10-07)
- [ ] **Write down questions and take notes / 写下问题并记录回答**
  - Prepare the questions you want to discuss. During the visit, take notes and ask for clarification when an explanation is unclear.
  - 提前写下想讨论的问题；就诊时记录回答，听不明白时要求解释。
  - Type: action · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Talking With Your Doctor](https://medlineplus.gov/talkingwithyourdoctor.html) (Summary; reviewed 2026-10-07)
- [ ] **Confirm what happens after the visit / 确认就诊后的下一步**
  - Ask for written next steps, the timing of any follow-up, and whom to contact with questions. Follow the treating clinician's instructions.
  - 询问书面的后续步骤、复诊安排，以及有问题时联系谁；遵循治疗医生的要求。
  - Type: question · Evidence: within_review_interval
  - Sources: [Be Prepared for Your Visit, Step 6](https://www.cms.gov/marketplace/outreach-and-education/downloads/c2c-prepare-for-your-visit.pdf) (PDF pages 4-7 (one-based); reviewed 2026-10-07); [Talking With Your Doctor](https://medlineplus.gov/talkingwithyourdoctor.html) (Summary; reviewed 2026-10-07)

## Questions for the office

- I'm a new patient arranging a visit. Do you accept new patients, and how should I book?
- Which clinician and location will I see? Which documents or prior records should I bring?
- I was already treated for a fracture and am arranging follow-up. Do I need a referral, and how should my existing records be sent?
- Could you arrange a Mandarin interpreter? Please confirm how this will work for my appointment.

## Questions for the insurer

- My exact plan and network are [plan/network]. Is [clinician] at [location] in this network?
- For the planned service [service], what coverage conditions, referral requirements, or prior authorization apply?
- What deductible or other cost sharing may apply? Can you provide a reference number for this conversation?

Project-written question templates. They ask for confirmation and do not state provider or insurer requirements.

This guide organizes public information. It does not diagnose, book an appointment, determine clinical urgency, or guarantee coverage. No personal medical information is required.
